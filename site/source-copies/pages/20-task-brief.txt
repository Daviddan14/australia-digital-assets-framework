Australia's Digital Assets Framework Act: What It Means for Tokeniser

Write a plain language explainer of Australia's Digital Assets Framework Act and what it means for issuers and for Tokeniser.

Brief

The Corporations Amendment (Digital Assets Framework) Act 2026 received Royal Assent on 8 April 2026 and commences on 9 April 2027. It brings digital asset platforms and tokenised custody platforms under ASIC licensing, in Redbelly's home market. Holders and issuers are asking what it changes. This explainer sets out the rules in plain language and shows where Redbelly's built in compliance fits.

This is a general explainer, not legal advice. Shortlisted entries are sent to the Redbelly team for review before anything is published.

Scope of work
Explain what the Act is and why it was introduced.
Set out who needs a licence and who does not.
Give the timeline, including key dates before and after 9 April 2027.
Explain what changes for issuers who tokenise assets through a platform like Tokeniser.
Show where Redbelly's compliance design lines up with the new rules, and where it is unclear.
Open with a clear statement that the piece is general information and not legal advice.
Write a short plain language summary the DAO can share with the community.
Deliverables
All are required
1.
The explainer, as a Google Doc, Word file or PDF. Length is your call, but it should be readable in one sitting.
2.
A short community summary (an X thread or a few paragraphs).
3.
A source list with a link for every factual claim, using the Act itself and ASIC material wherever possible.
Quality benchmarks
Every factual claim has a working source link, with primary sources (the Act, ASIC) preferred over news coverage.
Someone with no legal background understands who is affected and when.
Clear separation between what the law says and the writer's analysis.
Neutral tone, no claims that Redbelly or Tokeniser is already compliant unless a source says so.
Failure criteria
Any one fails the submission
Missing the "not legal advice" statement.
Any statement that Redbelly, Tokeniser or any issuer is licensed or compliant without a source.
Wrong dates, thresholds or licence requirements.
Any factual claim without a source, or with a source that does not support it.
Price talk, investment advice or promises of returns.
AI generated text submitted without checking, including made up facts or sources.
Personal credit inside the deliverable (names, handles, signatures, watermarks or "made by" lines). Contributors are credited by the DAO once the work is accepted, not inside the work itself.
