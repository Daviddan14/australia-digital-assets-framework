FAQ

Common questions about Tokeniser and tokenisation answered.

What is Tokenisation?

+

Tokenisation records ownership of financial products and
services as digital tokens, and lets you program the rules of
the asset. Holders gain fractional ownership, direct custody,
atomic settlement, and 24/7 secondary trading through protocol
finance.

Why use Tokeniser?

+

Tokeniser gives you the benefits of tokenisation without the
technology project. Your team keeps familiar registry
operations, one platform handles everything from issuance to
settlement, and you gain a lower cost of capital, faster
raises, and new distribution reach.

Is Tokeniser regulated?

+

Tokeniser is built for Australia's digital asset laws as
public digital token infrastructure: governed under local law,
with eligibility and compliance programmed into the protocol.
Products issued through Tokeniser operate under an Asset
Issuer's existing licensing.

Can we use our own systems?

+

Customer configuration is flexible. Our API and MCP let you
integrate Tokeniser with your existing registry, or build a
custom investor portal.

How long does onboarding take?

+

You set the pace. Onboarding is staged, so you can start with
a first raise or a registry migration and add capabilities as
you're ready. Our practitioners guide each stage, and your
timeline follows your business.

What about compliance?

+

Designed to work within your existing licensing framework,
Tokeniser programs eligibility into every asset at the
protocol level: identity, KYC, AML, CTF, wholesale and
sophisticated investor tests, and TMD. Every holder is
verified and every transfer checked against the asset's rules,
automatically.
